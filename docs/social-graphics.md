# HAUS approved social graphics

Approved by Jacob on 2026-10-02. Use this direction by default for future HAUS tweet graphics and article covers.

## Canonical reference

- `public/brand-kit/approved-social-template/reference.svg` is the approved Docs are live layout, frozen before article-cover development.
- `public/brand-kit/approved-social-template/reference.png` is the rendered reference.
- Personal reusable template: `artifact-template-haus-social-graphics`.
- Generator: `scripts/brand-kit/tweet-template.cjs`.

## Visual rules

- White background, ink `#191919`, brand pink `#f2a8cd`, pale pink `#f8d4e5`.
- Grid: 100px spacing at 1600px canvas width, 1.25px ink strokes at 8.5% opacity.
- Oversized HV SMEGS Style 1 `Haus` background at 3.5% opacity. Capital H.
- Anton headlines in ink, with a pink HV SMEGS accent overlapping their lower edge where appropriate.
- Small HAUS logo at upper left. Identity labels `@hausdotfun` and `haus.fun` at lower left.
- Identity labels are 52px high, ink and pink, separated by 12px. All UI containers and identity labels have a subtle 4px radius, not capsule rounding.
- Show real UI captures proportionally, with a slight tilt (roughly 4 degrees) and an intentional crop at the outer canvas edge. Use thin, quiet browser bars with the relevant real domain.
- No pink offset backing behind UI captures, heavy shadows, glossy treatments, or extra decorative symbols.
- Keep one focal message per image and enough white space around it.

## Formats and export

- Standard announcement: 1600 × 900.
- Article cover: 1500 × 600 (5:2); a 3000 × 1200 PNG export may also be supplied.
- Deliver on a local HTML page with inline SVG artwork. Type, grid, branding, browser bars and pills must remain vector paths/shapes.
- Actual UI screenshots may be embedded as images inside the SVG. Embed their actual MIME type; several older `.png` screenshot files contain JPEG bytes.
- Preserve source screenshots and document screenshot provenance. Do not invent product activity, user messages or launch status.

The What is Haus cover should communicate token creation → a shared holder workspace → building together. Use the UI as supporting evidence, not a collage that overwhelms the title.
