# HAUS brand direction

## Thesis

**We are the devs.** Holders organize, create, vote, and ship. HAUS supplies the tools; the community is the team.

Homepage headline: **WE ARE THE DEVS.**
Supporting line: **Your coin. Built by its holders.**
Signature: **Built by us.**

## Visual system

- Warm paper #f4f0e9, ink #191919, pink #f2a8cd, and pale pink #f8d4e5.
- Angular lowercase h monogram traced to the supplied silhouette: sloped upper edge, asymmetric stems, no detached block. Reusable SVG in public/haus-mark.svg and React mark in components/haus-mark.tsx.
- Anton display headlines, Space Grotesk controls, strong scale changes and editorial spacing.
- Flat graphic poster, asymmetric monogram composition, starburst sticker, sharp frames, and printed character artwork.
- Top navigation and a four-column discovery grid, reducing to two columns on phones.
- No app chrome gradients, glossy pills, blue illumination, market ticker, or sidebar.
- Generated community websites retain independent visual themes.

## Copy

Keep labels direct. One sentence where context is needed. Do not repeat an action in explanatory subtext. Keep essential demo, payment, and verification states explicit.

## Product vocabulary

Explore / My haus / Proposals / Site studio.
Each community contains The room / Website proposals / Community fund.
The living room serves holders; the Team room is the proposed top-20 group.

## Domain and assets

haus.fun and displayed subdomains are illustrative, not verified owned domains. Community identity must use Solana cluster and mint address. Mock coins and vector characters are demonstration content.
